## Camera Mode Controller

Allows controlling camera modes.

Hold `Left Stick In` and press `X` to toggle camera mode. When enabled, you can cycle through camera modes by holding `Right Stick In` and pressing `D-Pad Left/Right`.

Hold `Right Stick In` and press `D-Pad Up` to toggle zoom mode. Zoom in with `X` and zoom out with `Y`. Pushing `Left Stick Up/Down` while zooming changes the zoom speed.

Disabling zoom mode makes the camera smoothly zoom back to its original zoom.

You can rotate the camera with `Right Stick`.
