## Camera Mode Controller

Allows controlling camera modes.

### Replay Camera Mode
Hold `Left Stick In` and press `X` to toggle replay camera mode. 

When enabled, you can cycle through camera modes by holding `Right Stick In` and pressing `D-Pad Left/Right`.

### Zoom Mode
When in replay camera mode, hold `Right Stick In` and press `D-Pad Up` to toggle zoom mode. 

Zoom in with `X` and zoom out with `Y`. Pushing `Left Stick Up/Down` while zooming changes the zoom speed.

Disabling zoom mode makes the camera smoothly zoom back to its original zoom.

---

You can rotate the replay camera with `Right Stick`.

When watching a MKTV replay, inputs that interact with the replay like `X` for zoom or `Stick/D-Pad` will only work if `ZR` is held, so that camera can be controlled without interacting with the replay. You can still slow down the replay by holding `B` without hodling `ZR`.
