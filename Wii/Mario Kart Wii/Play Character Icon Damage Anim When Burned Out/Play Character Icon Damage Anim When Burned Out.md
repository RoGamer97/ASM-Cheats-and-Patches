## Play Character Icon Damage Anim When Burned Out

When burning out at the beginning of the race, the character icon will do the damage animation like other Mario Kart titles.

<details>
<summary>NTSC-U</summary>

```
C27E1AE0 00000003
80030008 740C0004
41820008 60000001
60000000 00000000
```
</details>
