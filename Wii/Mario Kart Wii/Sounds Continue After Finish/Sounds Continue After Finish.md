## Sounds Continue After Finish

After finishing a race/battle, sound effects will continue to play, except character voices, unless if using the Character Voices Play After Finish code. Cool when used with Course Music Continues After Finish (No Result Music) code.

<details>
<summary>NTSC-U</summary>

```
04704A24 60000000
```
</details>

<details>
<summary>PAL</summary>

```
0470B3C8 60000000
```
</details>
