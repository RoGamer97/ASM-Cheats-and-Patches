## Dead/Inactive Bomb Cars Are Visible

Makes it so dead/inactive Bomb Cars are visible. After a Bomb Car explodes, it is still moving, and will eventually respawn. With this code, dead/inactive Bomb Cars are visible and blinking, allowing you to avoid them, since they can respawn in front of you, causing you to hit them.
