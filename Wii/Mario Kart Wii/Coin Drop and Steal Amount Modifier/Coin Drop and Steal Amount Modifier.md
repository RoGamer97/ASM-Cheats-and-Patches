## Coin Drop and Steal Amount Modifier

Allows you to modify the amount of coins that are dropped or stolen. 

<details>
<summary>NTSC-U</summary>

XX: Drop Amount
YY: Steal Amount

```
04534FCC 38A000XX
04535080 38C000YY
```
</details>

<details>
<summary>PAL</summary>

XX: Drop Amount
YY: Steal Amount

```
04539B14 38A000XX
04539BC8 38C000YY
```
</details>
