## Items Can Land from Drop in Time Trials.md

Items can land on the floor when dropped in Time Trials instead of vanishing/poofing when touching the ground. If you want the opposite, modify it to 38600001, items will poof when touching the ground in races like Time Trials.

<summary>NTSC-U</summary>

```
04787C3C 38600000
```
</details>
