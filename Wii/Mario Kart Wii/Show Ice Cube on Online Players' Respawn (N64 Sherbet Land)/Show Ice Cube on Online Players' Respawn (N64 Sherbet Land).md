## Show Ice Cube on Online Players' Respawn (N64 Sherbet Land)

Makes the ice cube that appears on local player's karts or CPU's karts when respawning in N64 Sherbet Land also appear on online players, just like it does for CPUs offline. Basically a game "fix"
